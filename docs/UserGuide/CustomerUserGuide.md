# Customer User Guide

Welcome! This guide walks a **Customer** through every feature of the Inventory Monitoring System v2 — from creating an account to placing and tracking orders.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Create an Account
1. Open the site and click **Sign Up**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Email** — must be `@gmail.com` or `@my.cspc.edu.ph`.
   - **Password** — at least 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 number, and 1 special character.
   - **Full Name**, **Address**, **Contact Number** (auto-formatted to `+63…`).
3. Click **Register**. The system sends a 6-digit OTP to your phone via SMS.
4. Enter the OTP on the verification page to activate your account.

> Tip: You can also click **Sign Up with Google** — a customer account is auto-created and your email is verified instantly, but you'll still need to verify your phone.

### 1.2 Log In
1. Go to **Login**.
2. Enter your email and password (or click **Continue with Google**).
3. After 3 wrong attempts you'll be locked out for 3 minutes — wait and try again.
4. On success you'll be taken to the **Shop**.

### 1.3 Forgot Password
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen.
2. Enter your email — you'll receive a 6-digit code by email.
3. Type the code on the verification page.
4. Set a new password.

## 2. Browsing the Shop

`Shop` is your home screen as a customer.

- **Search** — type in the search bar to filter products by name or SKU.
- **Category Filter** — click a category chip to narrow the list.
- **Product Card** shows: image, name, price, stock status, and any "Customizable" badge.
- Click a product card to see full details.

### 2.1 Add to Cart
On any product, click **Add to Cart**:
- Choose quantity.
- The system checks stock — if you exceed it, you'll see *"Insufficient stock!"*.
- A confirmation appears and the **cart badge** in the navbar updates.

---

## 3. Customizing a Product

If a product has the **Customize** button, you can design your own version.

1. Open the product → click **Customize**.
2. The **Customization Studio** opens. You can:
   - Add **Text** (₱50 each).
   - Add **Shapes** (₱30 each).
   - Add **Logos** (₱150 each).
   - Toggle **LED Lighting** (+₱500).
   - Pick from the allowed **Textures** for that product.
3. The live price updates as you edit.
4. Click **Save Design** to keep it under **My Designs**, or **Add Customized to Cart** to send it straight to the cart.

> Staff and Admins are automatically notified the first time you save a brand-new design.

### 3.1 My Designs
- Click **My Designs** in the navbar.
- You'll see every design you've saved, with a preview image.
- From here you can **Reopen** to edit, **Add to Cart**, or **Delete**.

---

## 4. Your Cart

Open the cart from the navbar.

- Each line shows: image, name, quantity, unit price, and a remove button.
- Change the **quantity** input to update — stock is re-checked.
- The same product with **different customizations** appears as separate lines.

### 4.1 Checkout
1. Tick the checkboxes next to the items you want to buy (you can checkout a subset).
2. Click **Checkout**.
3. The system:
   - Verifies stock one more time.
   - Creates an **Order** with a unique number like `ORDR-XXXX`.
   - Decrements stock.
   - Removes the checked-out items from your cart (others stay).
4. You're redirected to **My Orders** with a success message.

---

## 5. Tracking Your Orders

Open **My Orders** from the navbar.

| Status | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pending** | Just submitted, awaiting review. |
| **Approved** | Staff/Admin accepted. |
| **Processing** | Being prepared. |
| **Ready for Pickup** | You can collect it. |
| **Completed** | Picked up / delivered. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you or staff (with reason). |

### 5.1 Cancel an Order
- Click **Cancel** on a `Pending` or `Approved` order.
- You won't be able to cancel once it moves to `Processing`.

### 5.2 View Receipt
- Click **View Receipt** on any order to see the full breakdown.
- The receipt includes a **barcode** of your order number.
- You can print the page or save it as PDF using your browser's print dialog.

---

## 6. Notifications

A bell icon in the navbar shows your unread count.

- Click the bell to open the dropdown of recent notifications.
- Click **View All** for the full notifications page.
- Use **Mark all as read** or click a single notification to mark it read.
- Delete a notification with the trash icon.

You'll receive notifications when:
- Your order status changes.
- A design you submitted is reviewed.

---

## 7. Profile

Click your avatar in the top right → **Profile**.

You can update:
- Full name, address, contact number, gender.
- Profile photo.
- Educational fields (degree, year, section) — optional.

> Changing your contact number will trigger re-verification via SMS OTP.

---

## 8. Settings

Click your avatar → **Settings**.

- **Enable Notifications** — toggle off to stop receiving in-app notifications. (You'll still receive transactional emails like password resets.)

---

## 9. Logging Out

Click your avatar → **Logout**. Your session is invalidated and you cannot use the browser's back button to return to protected pages.

---

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does the same product appear twice in my cart?**
It has two different customizations. Each unique design is a separate line.

**My OTP didn't arrive.**
Click **Resend Code** on the verification page. Make sure your phone has SMS reception and the number you entered is correct.

**Can I change my email?**
Email is fixed at registration. Contact support if you need it changed.

**Why was my order cancelled with a reason?**
Staff/Admin can reject orders that violate policy (e.g. stock errors). The reason appears on the order detail page.
